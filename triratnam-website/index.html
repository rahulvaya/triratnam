About the Property

A modern address with everyday comfort

Welcome to Triratnam Elite Stay — a newly built residential apartment building offering thoughtfully designed 1 BHK and 2 BHK homes in the heart of Sector 4, Hiran Magri, Udaipur. Whether you are a working professional, a small family, or relocating to the city, our apartments are ready to move in with all the essentials taken care of.

Each home features quality flooring, modular kitchens, large windows for natural light, and balconies with open city views. The building is secured 24×7 with guard surveillance and equipped with a lift and uninterrupted water supply.
